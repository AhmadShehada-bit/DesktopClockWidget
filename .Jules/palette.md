## 2026-08-30 - Added Tooltips to Icon-Only Buttons
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons often lack context, hindering accessibility for screen reader users and those seeking clarification on UI controls. Adding `ToolTip` properties drastically improves the UX.
**Action:** Always verify that buttons lacking descriptive text have an appropriate `ToolTip` attribute or ARIA label to ensure context is available to all users.
