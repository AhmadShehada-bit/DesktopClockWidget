## 2023-10-27 - Add tooltips to icon-only buttons
**Learning:** In a WPF/C# application, the `ToolTip` property is an effective way to improve accessibility and UX for icon-only or ambiguous buttons without altering the visual layout.
**Action:** Always check for icon-only buttons (like `+`, `-`, `...`, `Choose...`) and assign descriptive `ToolTip` text to them.
